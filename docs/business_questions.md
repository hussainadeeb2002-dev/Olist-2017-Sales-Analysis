## Business Problem

Olist's executive team needs a comprehensive understanding of its sales performance in 2017. While overall revenue provides a high-level view of business performance, the company needs to understand the key factors driving revenue growth, how customer acquisition and order behavior contribute to performance, where sales and customers are geographically concentrated, and which product categories generate the most value and sales volume.

The objective of this analysis is to evaluate Olist's overall sales performance, identify the primary drivers of revenue, examine geographic and product-level performance, and uncover patterns that can help support data-driven business and strategic decisions.

## Business Questions

1. What was Olist's total revenue in 2017?
2. What was the monthly revenue in 2017?
3. What was the change in monthly revenue during 2017?
4. What was the change in total monthly orders during 2017?
5. What was the change in Average Order Value (AOV) during 2017?
6. What was the change in the number of unique customers placing orders each month during 2017?
7. Were the customers placing orders in 2017 primarily new or repeat customers?
8. Which states contributed the most to Olist's unique customer base in 2017?
9. Did the states that contributed the most customers also contribute the most revenue in 2017?
10. Which product categories contributed the most to Olist's total revenue in 2017?
11. Did the product categories that generated the most revenue also have the highest sales volume in 2017?

## Business Goal

Evaluate Olist's 2017 sales performance, identify the key drivers of revenue, and analyze customer, geographic, and product-level patterns to uncover insights that support data-driven business decisions.Evaluate overall sales performance, identify revenue trends, and uncover opportunities to improve business growth through data-driven insights.
